See opportunitiesGet in touch

How it works

Real projects. Clear expectations.

These collaborations are designed for people who want practical experience, portfolio material and participation in real product work. They are not paid positions.

There is no fixed schedule, no guaranteed future hiring and no requirement to already have professional experience. Scope and availability are agreed before each collaboration begins.

Before applying

This is not an employment position or internship. Participation is project-based, unpaid and intended for learning, portfolio building and practical collaboration. Only join if this format genuinely makes sense for you.
